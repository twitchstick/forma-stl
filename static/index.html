Built for Unraid

Your database and uploads live in /data. Your existing model share is mounted read-only at /library. Scanning creates catalog entries without moving or changing the original files.

Set FORMA_USERNAME and FORMA_PASSWORD on your container to enable sign-in. Use your LAN or a secured reverse proxy for access.

Website imports

Public pages with Open Graph metadata can supply titles, descriptions, and photos. Login-only or blocked pages need manual details. Importing a link does not download STL files; upload files you’ve downloaded or scan your server folder.

YOUR SAVES, AT HOME

Import from website

Import models from MakerWorld, Printables, Cults3D, and Thangs. Your signed-in website session stays in Safari. Review each batch before adding it to your catalog.

Safari · MakerWorld

Drag the button below to Safari’s Favorites bar; replace your old bookmark to get the updated gallery capture. Open your MakerWorld collection while signed in, then click the bookmark. Read selected collections or capture the models loaded on the page, review your selection, and send it here.

Send MakerWorld to Forma

If the direct handoff is blocked, export the catalog JSON from the bookmark panel and upload it below. Capturing loaded page cards may include only part of a collection; scroll and capture again, or use Read selected collections.

Safari · Printables, Cults3D & Thangs

Drag this bookmark to Safari’s Favorites bar; replace your old bookmark to get the updated gallery capture. Open a model, collection, or saved list on any of these sites and click it. Give the collection a name, scroll to load more models, and capture again before sending your selection.

Send website to Forma

Captures photos, titles, source links, and collection names from loaded cards. Individual model pages can also include descriptions, creators, and tags. This is a catalog capture, not automatic account sync. Download STL/3MF files normally and add them through Forma or your watched folder. JSON exports from all four sites can be uploaded below.

Optional · Chrome / Edge browser companion

1. Install the browser companion

Download and extract the ZIP. In Chrome or Edge, open Extensions, turn on Developer mode, choose Load unpacked, and select the extracted folder. Click the Forma extension icon to open the importer.

2. Pair this Forma server

In the companion, enter this server’s address and a one-use pairing code. Codes expire after ten minutes. No MakerWorld password is needed in Forma.

Pairing allows this browser to stage Import from websites and files. It cannot approve imports, delete entries or edit your existing library.

Daily schedule

Runs while Forma is running, even when your browser is closed. Missed checks run once after Forma restarts. Site sign-in, verification, or lazy-loaded content may require a Safari refresh. Partial checks are labeled clearly and never remove local models.
